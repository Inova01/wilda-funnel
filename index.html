Her Story

Why I Talk About Business, Money & Legacy

I've always believed that ambition should lead to something bigger than simply earning more.

  • 01 We work hard.
  • 02 We build businesses.
  • 03 We support families.
  • 04 We create opportunities.

But one question is often forgotten:

How do we protect what we are working so hard to build?

My goal is to create conversations around business, money, leadership, protection, and legacy in a way that feels practical, relatable, and empowering.

Because success is not only about what you earn. It is also about what you build, what you protect, and what you leave behind.

For Entrepreneurs

Your Business Depends on More Than Revenue.

Two lists sit on every business owner's desk. Most of us only ever finish the first one.

What we spend our time on

  • Customers.
  • Sales.
  • Marketing.
  • Growth.

Entrepreneurs spend a lot of time thinking about these — and they matter. This is the work that creates revenue.

What strong businesses also think about

  • Risk.
  • Continuity.
  • Cash flow.
  • People.
  • Protection.

And what happens when things don't go according to plan.

The Business & Wealth Check-In

7 questions every ambitious professional or entrepreneur should ask about what they are building.

  • Is my income built on one source — or several?
  • If I could not work for 90 days, what would happen next?
  • How much of my business depends entirely on me?
  • Who is counting on the money I bring in?
  • What am I setting aside consistently, and why?
  • Where might the gaps in my protection be?
  • What do I want to still be standing in twenty years?
